Julian of Norwich's original text is in the public domain.

The Middle English base text is the Long Text of *A Revelation of Love* as preserved
in **Sloane 2499** (British Library), consulted through the freely available scholarly
edition of the Middle English Texts Series (TEAMS/METS), with reference to the Paris
manuscript (BnF fonds anglais 40) where readings diverge.

First edition, 2026.

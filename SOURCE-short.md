# SOURCE-short.md
## Julian of Norwich — *A Vision Showed to a Devout Woman* (Short Text)
### Base Text: Amherst Manuscript (British Library, Additional 37790)

---

This file holds the **Middle English Short Text** — Julian's earlier account of the
1373 showings, the version the project is producing **first**. It is the ONLY text the
Short Text translation is made from.

**Source:** Julia Bolton Holloway's transcription of the Amherst manuscript at
<https://www.umilta.net/amherst.html> — the standard freely available Middle English
Short Text. (The Short Text survives in this single manuscript.) Mark copies the Middle
English by hand; Claude normalises it into a clean reading text.

**The Amherst transcription is *diplomatic*** — it mimics the manuscript: scribal
abbreviations shown as spaced letters (*"Secou n de"* = *Seconde*, *"d omi ni"* =
*domini*), yogh written as "3", line-end marks "=/", capitulum braces "{", and heavy
original capitalisation. Our normalisation:

- **Expand scribal abbreviations** to full words; log any non-obvious expansion.
- **Restore clean letter-forms:** thorn **þ** and yogh **ȝ** are *preserved* (this is a
  manuscript-faithful Middle English edition), but the diplomatic spacing/marks are
  removed. *(This reverses the modern-letter-form ruling, which applies only to the
  Long Text / Crampton phase — see `NOTES.md`.)*
- **Remove line-end marks** ("=/") and rejoin words; do not otherwise alter spelling.
- **Regularise capitalisation** lightly toward sentence case; log if a capital seems
  meaningful.

**Letter-forms note:** because þ and ȝ are retained here, the Module 7
special-character check applies again at build/QA time.

**Capture conventions (per section):**
- Delimiter `## Chapter N` (the Short Text is conventionally divided into ~25 short
  sections; numbering follows the standard scholarly division and is **independent of
  the Long Text's 86 chapters**).
- Keep editorial observations out of the running text; move them to `NOTES.md`.
- Reflow to running paragraphs; preserve words and spelling.

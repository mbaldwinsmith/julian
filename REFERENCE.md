## Grace Warrack (1901) — Modernised Edition
### REFERENCE ONLY — NEVER TRANSLATE FROM THIS FILE

---

⚠️ **This is a predecessor's translation, not the source.**

Grace Warrack's 1901 edition is a lightly modernised rendering of Sloane 2499. It is
public domain (freely available via Project Gutenberg, CCEL, and Internet Archive)
and useful as a **sanity check**: where your own reading of the Middle English
diverges sharply from Warrack's, that divergence is worth pausing over. Sometimes
you'll be right and she missed something; sometimes she caught a sense you've
overlooked.

But Warrack already made thousands of interpretive decisions, smoothed Julian's
syntax, and modernised her vocabulary. Translating from Warrack would mean inheriting
her choices wholesale and producing a translation of a translation. The STYLEGUIDE
forbids this.

**Workflow:** Consult this file *after* drafting from `SOURCE.md`, never before.
